Questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your basket and tap "Send order on WhatsApp". We reply to confirm stock, the total and the delivery time.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Yes. We send our MoMo number when we confirm your order. You can also pay cash on collection.

What is the minimum for wholesale prices?

It depends on the product, usually one carton or one bag. Send your list and we will quote the best price.

What if an item is out of stock?

We tell you before you pay and suggest a similar product, or remove it from your order.
